Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 30 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how SGVR TECH (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Indiyoura mobile application and related services (the “App”).

1. Who we are

Indiyoura is operated by SGVR TECH and is intended for users in India. If you have a question about this policy or your information, contact us at sgvrtech1@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account and profile

When you create or update an account, we collect:

  • First name, last name, and email address
  • Phone number and one-time password (OTP) verification status
  • Date of birth, gender, and user type or role
  • State, city, pincode, and address
  • Profile image
  • Password, stored only in hashed form

2.2 Matrimony profile

If you use matrimony features, we collect the details you choose to provide, which may include name, gender, age, date of birth, photos, “about me”, education, occupation, income (with a visibility setting), caste, subcaste, gothra, family details, work location, hometown, hobbies, languages, height, blood group, marital status, and optional social links (such as Instagram, WhatsApp, or LinkedIn) with visibility settings.

2.3 Listings and seller contact details

If you create a listing (for example real estate, furniture, vehicles, food, jewellery, or similar marketplace content), we collect the listing name, description, price, images, videos, documents, category details, address or map link, and the seller name, phone number, and email you provide.

2.4 Social content

If you use social features, we store posts, comments, reactions, follows, moments, jobs, and notifications you create or receive, and reports you submit about posts or listings (reporter account and reason).

2.5 Subscriptions

If you subscribe to a matrimony, real estate, jewellery, or similar plan, we store the plan name, price, any discount or coupon code, start and end dates, whether the subscription is active, and a payment status. The App does not collect card numbers, UPI IDs, or bank account details. If a payment gateway is added later, this policy will be updated to describe what that provider collects.

2.6 Temple event bookings

If you book a temple event slot, we store your account, the event, the slot, and the booking status. Booking does not collect payment card or bank details in the App.

2.7 Reports and feedback

If you report an issue or a safety concern, we store your account, a description, and a problem type (such as bug, feedback, feature request, or other). Child-safety reports are handled as described in our Child Safety Standards.

2.8 Device and usage information

When you use the App we may receive technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, and IP address, used to operate the service, keep it secure, and diagnose problems. The App may use your location, with your permission, to show nearby shops. The App may use your camera and photo library, with your permission, for profile pictures, posts, and listings.

3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and manage your account, including login and phone verification
  • Provide matrimony, marketplace, social, jewellery, temple, and subscription features you choose to use
  • Show listings, profiles, and nearby places
  • Respond to reports, feedback, and support requests
  • Protect users, including child-safety review and enforcement
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve the App
  • Send service messages, and promotional messages where permitted

4. Service providers

We use service providers to run the App. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Database hosting (MongoDB) for account and app data
  • Amazon Web Services (S3) for photos, videos, and other files you upload
  • Twilio to send SMS one-time passwords to your phone number
  • Google Maps for place search and maps
  • Translation services when content is translated in the App

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing

Information you post in a public area of the App (such as a public profile, post, or listing) can be seen by other users according to the visibility settings of that feature. Seller phone and email on a listing may be shown to users who view that listing.

We may share information:

  • With the service providers listed above
  • If required by law, court order, or a government or law-enforcement request
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of users, including reports of child sexual abuse material
  • If the business is sold or transferred, in which case this policy continues to apply to that information

6. Retention and account deletion

We keep information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the App, meet legal duties, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms. You can request deletion of your account in the App or by emailing sgvrtech1@gmail.com. Some records may be kept where the law requires it, including safety reports and records we must preserve for investigations.

7. Security

We use administrative and technical safeguards, including hashed passwords, to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Indiyoura is not directed at children. Accounts are intended for users who are at least 18 years old. We do not knowingly collect account information from children. We have a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse and exploitation. How to report a concern, and how we handle those reports, is described in our Child Safety Standards. You can also email sgvrtech1@gmail.com.

9. Your choices

  • You can update profile details in the App
  • You can control visibility of certain matrimony fields and account privacy settings where the App provides them
  • You can deny camera, photo, or location permission in your device settings; related features will then be limited
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion by contacting us at the email below

10. Third-party sites

The App may link to websites or services we do not control, including maps and social links you or other users add. This policy does not cover those sites.

11. Changes

We may update this policy by posting a new version on this page and changing the “Last Updated” date. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
